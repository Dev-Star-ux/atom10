RiskSpectrum PSA, the RiskSpectrum risk-and-reliability suite, MELCOR, and SAPHIRE are licensed nuclear-safety programs. The installers are not public, so they are not in this folder.

RiskSpectrum PSA
  Commercial PSA code. Lloyd's Register sold the product line to Total Specific Solutions in 2022. It is now sold as RiskSpectrum.
  Product page: https://www.riskspectrum.com/riskspectrum-psa
  Registered downloads: https://downloads.riskspectrum.com/RiskSpectrumPSA/
  Registration: https://downloads.riskspectrum.com/RiskSpectrumPSA/Register.aspx
  Support: support@riskspectrum.com

Risk and reliability software
  That name is the RiskSpectrum product family (PSA, plus the related risk, reliability, and availability tools on riskspectrum.com). It is the same vendor as RiskSpectrum PSA, not a separate free program.
  Family page: https://www.riskspectrum.com/

  The U.S. NRC counterpart for fault-tree and event-tree PRA is SAPHIRE, from Idaho National Laboratory. The executable also requires an NRC agreement. The public NUREG/CR-7039 manuals are in SAPHIRE_public_manuals.
  Request the code: email safetycodes@nrc.gov
  NRC page: https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/regulatory/research/obtainingcodes

MELCOR
  Sandia severe-accident code for the NRC. The executable is distributed only after an NRC non-disclosure agreement, then a Sandia download login and a per-computer license.
  Domestic organizations: email a signed NDA to safetycodes@nrc.gov. After NRC and Sandia approval, the code is downloaded from https://snltransfer.sandia.gov. License keys go to MLR@sandia.gov.
  Distribution page: https://www.sandia.gov/MELCOR/code-distribution/
  NRC obtaining-codes page: https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/regulatory/research/obtainingcodes
  The public MELCOR 2.2 r2023.0 manuals are in MELCOR_public_manuals. Those PDFs are the user and reference manuals, not the program.

What is in this folder
  SCRAM: GPLv3 open-source PRA solver from https://github.com/SCRAM-NG/scram (release 0.17.0). This is a fault-tree and event-tree tool you can run without an NRC or RiskSpectrum license. Documentation: https://scram-pra.org/
  SAPHIRE_public_manuals: NUREG/CR-7039 Volumes 1-7, public NRC PDFs.
  MELCOR_public_manuals: public NRC PDFs of the MELCOR 2.2 r2023.0 primer, user's guide, and reference manual.
